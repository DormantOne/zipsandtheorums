Boolean

Integer counts

Use popcount or ripple-carry bitplanes. PIA parity alone cannot produce ordinary counts.

Guardrails

Track P-pattern cost, cap run fragmentation, canonicalize after XOR, and decompress when structure disappears.

Bottom line: packed/Four-Russians is the general engine. Corrected RSBE/PIA is an exact accelerator for visibly structured blocks, never a mandatory replacement.
