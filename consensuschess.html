What this experiment can — and cannot — establish

No weight sharing: each of the 32 worker ganglia maintains independent trainable parameters and independent optimizer state. A developmental-initialization condition starts nodes from similar but not identical weights, while the cold-start control uses fully independent random initialization.

Participation is audited: ordinary relays forward unchanged signals unless they have two partial reports to combine; the separate active-relay condition forces more ganglia to apply independently learned neural transformations. Compositional bias is explicit: a fixed spanning-tree routing protocol combines neural reports two at a time. There is no hard-coded XOR formula in any neural operation, but the tree and staged curriculum are intentionally favorable to parity. Training uses supervised, centrally orchestrated reverse-mode differentiation across the sparse operations. Reverse-edge hop counts are instrumented, but training gradients are not actually sent over delayed or lossy network links. Only forward task reports, voting, heartbeats, and election traffic have simulated message timing. This is not teacher-free evolution or decentralized training across machines.

Consensus is governance, not the calculator: a separate organ collects routed reports and arranges a 2-of-3 vote. The three voters receive correlated learned predictions, not independent full solutions. Destroying that organ suspends decisions; replacing it does not erase the worker ganglia's learned weights. A 17-of-32 worker majority is required to elect the successor. If no quorum arrives before a deadline, the answer is ABSTAIN.

Success criterion: mastery of all 64 balanced six-bit patterns, unseen full eight-bit combinations (although each individual input has appeared in smaller training examples) on all 256 patterns, sensor-withholding ablations, and survival of a consensus failure. The ten-bit challenge adds two previously unseen sensing locations. The battery shows failures and plateaus, rather than automatically promoting the best trial to a proof of general intelligence. High scores on two-bit-subset curricula are evidence for highly task-shaped compositional learning, not spontaneous invention of routing or a general consensus algorithm.
